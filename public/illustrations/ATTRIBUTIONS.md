## Verification & Integrity Policy

* **No AI Generated Placeholders:** Every illustration in this repository is an authentic vector asset created by open-source artists.
* **Duplicate Detection:** Assets are hashed via SHA-256 to ensure no artificial duplicates exist.
* **Format:** Clean, scalable vector graphics (SVG) with standardized coordinate systems.
